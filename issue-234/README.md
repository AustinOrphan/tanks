# Issue #234 — palette candidates

`palette-candidates-as-rings.png` — drawn as rings on the arena felt `#2f6d4f`, alpha-composited at the
shipped 0.85, which is what a player actually sees. Rows, top to bottom:

1. **shipped identity** — cyan, orange, vermillion, violet
2. **proposed identity** — the measured optimum under a 3:1 ground-contrast floor
3. **shipped teams** — red, blue, green
4. **proposed teams** — the measured optimum under the same floor

The proposed rows score better on colour-vision robustness and ground contrast, and look markedly
weaker: forcing every ring brighter than the felt pushes the whole palette into pastels.
